# Surface brief: public home page (and the system the whole app inherits)

Mode: Persuade (home), Operate (portals later, same world, quieter).
Audience: parents and prospective families in Harare, mostly on phones; secondary: school buyers judging the suite. Action: apply, book a visit, pay fees, enter a portal. Constraints (PRODUCT.md): logos, photos, routes, copy, i18n and backend untouched.
Unresolved: the school's official colours; real exam results, fees and term dates are not supplied and must not be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The school is a dry-stone wall in the Great Zimbabwe tradition: coursed granite, a chevron band at the crown, and gateways you walk through. It refuses the template's hero carousel, quick-link bar and card grid. Each audience enters by its own gateway.

OWN-WORLD: Granite ground in two states, sunlit stone (low-chroma warm grey) and shadow stone (blue-black charcoal). Brand navy and blue taken from the supplied logo carry action; lichen ochre is the only marker colour. Square corners, hairline dry joints, slabs with real offset shadows. Archivo for headings, Public Sans for text. The chevron band is the signature ornament.

STORY: A parent sees a real Harare classroom built into a stone wall, knows this is a school with local roots, finds Apply, Visit, Pay fees and Portal as four gateways, and acts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shadow-stone wall with the classroom photograph laid through irregular courses of stone blocks, the school name set large on a lintel slab bottom-left, and four gateway slabs along the foot (Apply, Visit, Pay fees, Portal). The chevron band crosses beneath the nav. Primary action Apply sits on the left gateway.

FORM: Great Zimbabwe masonry, a user-chosen grounded alternate, position 3 of 7; seed:903a836209bd436b (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
